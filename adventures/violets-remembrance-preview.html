Violet’s Remembrance

A gentle companion adventure about evidence, memory, and the freedom to set a burden down. Explore Brighton’s public places at your own pace—with an optional supply and vintage-shopping loop, a sober remembrance trail, or a fully remote route using maps.

Self-pacedSober by designShopping optionalRemote-friendly

Story boundary: This is a proposed companion experience, not a declaration that Violet dies in established canon. Real locations are settings; fictional clues are labelled as fiction. No private homes, personal tracking, or unverified claims about real people.
