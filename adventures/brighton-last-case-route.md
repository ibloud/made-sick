# Brighton companion adventure — supply, vintage & sober trail

**Status:** proposal / route content for review; not yet wired into the live adventure UI.  
**Story frame:** *Violet’s Revenge: The Last Case — Brighton* is a companion adventure about remembrance, evidence, and letting the dead rest. It does not declare Violet dead in established canon. Keep this route non-canon until approved.

## Route design

Offer two modes, so supply shopping is optional and the memorial walk remains gentle:

1. **Supply & style loop — North Laine / city centre.** Find a plain white candle, check for a field-hockey stick, and browse vintage clothes.
2. **Sober remembrance trail — St Nicholas / seafront / The Queery.** A self-guided walk with quiet pauses, no pub or alcohol purchase objectives, and a sober community-space option. People can skip any stop, leave early, or complete it remotely with Google Maps.

All shop inventory and opening hours can change. The listed retailers are leads, not guarantees; call or check their current listing before travelling.

- **Why here:** Its candle listing describes plain white household candles and altar/pillar candles.

**Story clue:** A white candle represents a light carried for remembrance, not proof of a supernatural claim. Do not light candles in public streets, parks, historic sites, or other places unless explicitly permitted; an unlit candle or phone-light alternative works just as well.

## Sober remembrance trail

This trail is deliberately **alcohol-free in its objectives**: no pub crawl, bar challenges, or alcohol purchases. It is not a clinical recovery service, and no participant needs to disclose why they prefer a sober route.

### Stop A — St Nicholas Church Gardens
- A historic burial-ground setting for a quiet pause about what is known, what is remembered, and what remains unknown.
- [Google Maps / Street View](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=St+Nicholas+Church+Gardens+Brighton)
- [Walking directions](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=St+Nicholas+Church+Gardens%2C+Brighton)

**Prompt:** Write one confirmed fact, one interpretation, and one thing you cannot know. Respect worshippers, mourners, graves, and posted rules; leave no objects behind.

### Stop B — Brighton seafront / West Pier viewpoint
- A public-facing place to reflect on release and the difference between closure and certainty. Stay on permitted paths and viewing areas; do not enter the West Pier structure.
- [Google Maps / Street View](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=West+Pier+viewpoint+Brighton)
- [Walking directions from St Nicholas Church Gardens](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=St+Nicholas+Church+Gardens%2C+Brighton&destination=West+Pier%2C+Brighton&travelmode=walking)

**Prompt:** Name one burden you are ready to set down, or simply pause without writing anything.

### Stop C — The Queery, Kemptown (optional sober-space finish)
- **Address:** 46 George Street, Brighton BN2 1RJ.
- The Queery describes itself as a sober community space, bookshop, café, and community venue. This is an optional destination, not a requirement to attend a meeting or share personal information.
- [Google Maps / Street View](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Queery+46+George+Street+Brighton)
- [The Queery](https://thequeery.nekoweb.org/)
- [TransSober drop-in information](https://transsober.org/drop-in-groups/)

Check the venue's current opening and event schedule before travelling. A venue being sober does not mean every event is suitable for every visitor; choose what feels right and leave whenever you wish.

## Practical accessibility and care

- Make every stop optional, with an estimated walk time, seating/rest opportunity, toilet information only when verified, and a clear way to shorten the route.
- Provide a remote mode with map links and text clues; no purchase or physical travel should be required to finish the story.
- Keep the supply loop separate from the remembrance trail. The hockey stick is not needed for the sober route.
- No grave rubbing, trespass, touching memorials, leaving offerings, or lighting open flames at public/heritage sites.
- Avoid alcohol-centred venues in the route design. Do not promise that every nearby business is alcohol-free; identify only spaces that explicitly describe themselves as sober.
- Mark access details, opening hours, inventory, route length, and Street View coverage as **check before visiting** until directly verified.
- Include a low-stimulation option: visit early, use headphones only where safe, skip crowds, and pause or end the trail at any point.
