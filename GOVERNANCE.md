# Governance draft

## Profile states

- **Editorial reference:** evidence-bounded public commentary. The person is not enrolled.
- **Applicant:** the creator has requested participation; nothing is public by default.
- **Verified participant:** identity control and selected public fields have been checked.
- **Ally:** an opted-in creator who supports the campaign without claiming illness.
- **Archived:** removed from discovery while preserving a minimal audit record where legally appropriate.

## Verification is field-specific

A check must identify what was reviewed, its source class, reviewer, and review date. A profile-level badge must never imply that every claim is independently true or professionally endorsed.

Acceptable labels include:

- Creator-controlled
- Publicly verified
- Self-reported
- Professional credential verified
- Sponsored
- Personal wellness experience

## Health and wellness

Made Sick does not diagnose, treat, or validate medical conditions. Creators are never required to disclose a diagnosis. Personal routines must be presented as personal experience and must not promise diagnosis, treatment, prevention, cure, or guaranteed outcomes.

Content involving health products, professional services, sponsorship, or affiliate relationships requires additional review and clear disclosure. Platform review does not replace independent legal advice.

## Consent and correction

- Enrollment requires an affirmative action by the creator or authorized representative.
- Every creator-controlled profile must provide a correction and removal route.
- Public references must be clearly distinguished from participants.
- A creator may leave the directory without surrendering their AT Protocol identity or source writing.
- Material disputes pause the affected verification label while reviewed.

## Private contact

Germ contact is optional and creator-enabled. Made Sick should not proxy, retain, or publish private message content. Blocking, abuse reporting, and contact permissions must remain available.
