Voting Edition

Your voice, where you are.

Choose the jurisdiction where you are eligible to vote. Your current location may be different. No GPS, account or street address is needed here. Selection stays on this page and is not saved.

Optional: suggest my current area

Only if you press the button below and allow browser location, your coordinates will be sent to BigDataCloud to suggest a country and U.S. state. Made Sick does not save them. This cannot establish your voting residence; confirm the selection yourself before showing resources.

Use the election authority for your voting residence to verify registration and status, dates and deadlines, polling places, early/absentee/mail options, assistance and accessibility. Ask your local office for the official candidate and office list, sample ballot, ballot questions or referendums, and local election administration. Made Sick does not generate a personalized ballot or reproduce live election dates.

Check the election year and update date on the official site. If a lookup is unavailable, contact that election office; missing data does not mean no election or no candidates. External lookup sites may ask for an address or postcode under their own privacy terms. We do not receive those entries.

Made Sick does not tell you who or what to vote for. These links go directly to official election resources. Eligibility and voting rules vary by jurisdiction.
