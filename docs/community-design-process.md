Recorded 22 October 2026 (UTC). Scope: homepage composition, illustration and scoped CSS; existing directory, join, Story Finder and project destinations remain linked.

## Human direction

Dominique requested a distinct identity rather than familiar AI website conventions, introduced Solai as a reference, selected the third displayed concept (black masthead and gig-poster layout), rejected the heavy purple treatment, approved the paper/charcoal/ochre/petrol palette, and requested educational disclosures and implementation. ## AI contribution

ChatGPT/Codex reviewed existing code and artwork, proposed three image-generated page concepts, researched colour references, proposed the approved palette, generated a colour-revised fictional studio illustration through OpenAI’s built-in image-generation tool, and wrote homepage HTML/CSS and this record. The exact image model/version was not exposed by that tool and is not asserted here. No Wazir output was used.

## Assets and limits

- Raster image text was deliberately removed. Headline, links and actions remain semantic HTML so they can reflow, be selected and be read by assistive technology.

## Reproduction brief for the new illustration

Edit the existing fictional community illustration. Preserve five people collaborating around a table, wheelchair user, guitarist, tabletop game, books, laptop, sleeping cat and plants. Remove all lettering. Use hand-inked screenprint style with warm paper #F4EBDD, charcoal #242321 and ochre #E8B949 dominant; deep petrol #285653 as support and muted plum #705568 only as tiny accents. Replace bright purple clothes/backgrounds with petrol, olive, ochre and warm neutrals. No claim of real people or a real event. The tool returned a 1672 × 941 raster; it was exported to WebP for delivery.
