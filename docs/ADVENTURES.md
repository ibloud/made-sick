## Future vetted listings

Planned, not implemented: events, accommodations and practical travel options. Current suggestions are not vetted or universally suitable.

Future listings should record:
- rest: seating, quiet spaces, breaks and toilets;
- physical access: steps, lifts, distances and facilities actually confirmed;
- sensory conditions: noise, lighting, crowds and quieter options;
- flexibility: cancellation, check-in, re-entry and early departure;
- costs and mandatory purchases, separated from optional spending;
- evidence for each field: source, reviewer, review date and unknowns;
- correction and withdrawal contact, review expiry and conflicts of interest.

“Sick-friendly” helps visitors choose using concrete details, without assigning a diagnosis or promising suitability for everyone. Exploration requires no health disclosure. Before accepting submissions, establish review ownership and correction/removal processes. Sponsorship cannot purchase a vetting outcome.
