# Made Sick diary pilot — review draft

Status: prepared for owner review; not published to pckt or Bluesky. This package proposes a pilot without a separate solver list. It does not amend the external SuperMe.ai note.

## Question

Does PIXIE help people participate, discover another artist, and return for the next diary—with manageable effort for everyone involved?

## Reader experience

The diary is the main experience. Each of four entries has one optional clue. Choose a single stop, follow any part of the route, or choose the entire tour. The same clue can be answered from home. No GPS check, purchase, account, public answer, or completion submission is required. Taking a break or skipping a stop does not lock later entries.

Approved word: LINK. Manchester supplies L; Köln supplies I. The final two stops supply N and K; their locations and dates remain unconfirmed. Planned first two publication dates are 11 and 14 October 2026, subject to clearance and owner approval.

Do not replace the diaries with a quest landing page or use PIXIE as an analytics detour.

## Publication workflow

1. Confirm the exact Made Sick pckt blog URL, publishing account, and actual diary source text.
2. Prepare one cleared diary and an original fallback entry. Record clearance separately for text, photographs, video, quotations, and case-study reuse. A public link is not permission to reuse an artist's media or imply affiliation.
3. Append the relevant optional clue below to each reviewed diary. Put answers after a visible spoiler warning or supported reveal control; verify the control in pckt rather than assuming custom scripts run.
4. Check the published preview on iPad: readable text, functional artist links, easy answer access, next-entry navigation, no required purchase, and the complete-tour choice.
5. Obtain owner's approval of the actual entry and final Bluesky copy before publishing.
6. After each stop, record aggregate observations and anonymous feedback counts. Review the two-stop pilot after Köln. Do not claim broad impact.

## Original fallback entry — ready for review

### One song, one small connection

You do not need to travel to join this adventure. Start with a song you already know. Notice one thing that holds your attention: a lyric, a sound, or the feeling it leaves behind.

If you have the energy, explore another artist through an official link in the cleared diary. If that link is not ready, choose a musician you already follow. You can listen quietly, share a thought with someone who welcomes it, or stop here.

The route remains available whether you choose one stop or the entire tour. There is no purchase and no need to report where you are.

Append the clue for the relevant stop. This original fallback makes no claim that a concert, visit, artist meeting, or personal diary event occurred. It needs the same owner review as the diary.

## Optional feedback prompt

If you want to reply on the diary's accompanying Bluesky post: did you try an outing or a from-home activity, discover another artist, or have a meaningful interaction? Did it fit your time and energy? Answer only what you want.

Replies are public. Please leave out health details, private locations, and other people's personal information. We will summarize counts without copying handles or quotations. Case-study quotation or identifiable reuse requires separate permission.
