Joining does not verify a diagnosis, legal identity, employment, authorship, or any other claim. It establishes identity control and affirmative directory participation only.

If you see a permission error after permissions change, use Log out and reconnect. OAuth grants are attached to the session; reconnecting is required to receive the updated permission set.

01

Authenticate

AT Protocol OAuth identifies the DID controlled by the participant. The browser SDK handles PKCE, DPoP, callback processing, and session storage.

02

Consent

The participant separately agrees to directory publication. Authentication alone never enrolls anyone.

03

Publish to your repo

Made Sick writes one org.made-sick.participant record to the participant's own repository. The participant can withdraw it from this page.
