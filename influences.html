Made Sick is an independent project. This page acknowledges people whose work has influenced how its creator thinks about technology, healthcare, entrepreneurship, creativity, and building practical systems that serve people.

Professional influence · 01

Mark Cuban

01

Why he is named here

Mark Cuban's work across entrepreneurship, technology, healthcare, media, and civic life has influenced the creator of Made Sick and the way this project thinks about practical problem-solving.

02

A mentor-like influence

The relationship described here is one of professional respect and learning from a leader whose work the creator follows closely. Public interaction and observation can be meaningful without becoming a formal advisory or business relationship.

03

Standing beside the work

This acknowledgment is about solidarity with the values and possibilities the creator sees in that body of work: better systems, practical access, and a better tomorrow for the people those systems are meant to serve.

Important boundary: Made Sick does not speak for Mark Cuban, Mark Cuban Companies, the Mark Cuban Foundation, or any of their companies or organizations. This page does not claim endorsement, funding, partnership, advisory status, or participation. It is an acknowledgment of personal professional influence and respect from the creator of Made Sick.

The principle

Learn from people. Keep your own voice.

Made Sick can recognize the people who helped shape its thinking while keeping authorship, accountability, and consent clear. Influence can be named without turning admiration into a claim that someone joined the project.

If a named relationship ever becomes formal—financial support, advisory work, technology support, institutional partnership, or explicit endorsement—Made Sick will describe that relationship separately and accurately.
