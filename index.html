Participation contract

Your story is not the price of admission.

Opt in. No creator-controlled profile without affirmative consent.

Choose the evidence. Identity, work, events, credentials, and lived experience are labeled separately.

Keep health personal. Wellness routines are personal experience—not diagnosis, treatment, or guaranteed outcomes.

Leave cleanly. Directory removal never requires surrendering an AT identity or creator-owned writing.

AT Proto, without the fog

What do I actually own?

Your identity +

An AT Protocol account has a portable identifier called a DID. Your readable handle can change; the underlying identity remains consistent.

Your writing +

pckt.blog can publish long-form work into your Personal Data Server using shared record formats. Made Sick reads approved records; it does not become their owner.

Your directory presence +

The directory is curated. Verification describes the evidence checked, not the truth of every statement a creator makes.

Your private messages +

Germ supplies a separate end-to-end encrypted channel. Private messages do not become public profile content.
