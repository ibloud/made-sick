Made Sick Sessions · Proposed pilot

Creator-owned stories, live.

An opt-in creator may pair a pckt.blog story with a public Streamplace conversation about the work, milestone, and practices that helped them continue.

No session is scheduled. No player loads—and no viewer data is sent to Streamplace—until an approved creator and stream are configured.

  1. 01
    Creator consents

    Identity, topic, guests, recording, reuse, and withdrawal terms are confirmed.

  2. 02
    Safety is assigned

    A named moderator, content note, resource plan, and escalation boundary are published.

  3. 03
    Rights are cleared

    Only owned, licensed, or authorized media may be broadcast.

  4. 04
    The stream is embedded

    The creator's Streamplace handle—not a copied video—is used on the profile.

AT Proto, without the fog

What do I actually own?

Your identity +

An AT Protocol account has a portable identifier called a DID. Your readable handle can change; the underlying identity remains consistent.

Your writing +

pckt.blog can publish long-form work into your Personal Data Server using shared record formats. Made Sick reads approved records; it does not become their owner.

Your directory presence +

The directory is curated. Verification describes the evidence checked, not the truth of every statement a creator makes.

Your private messages +

Germ supplies a separate end-to-end encrypted channel. Private messages do not become public profile content.
